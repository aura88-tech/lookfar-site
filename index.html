How it works

Three steps, and you don't need your phone.

01 · set

Pick your active hours

Reminders come only inside the hours you set. Choose how often: every 15, 30, 45 minutes, 1, 1.5 or 2 hours. The default is every hour.

02 · buzz

Feel the buzz, take the break

The watch vibrates and asks: time for a quick eye break? Say yes, and a 20–40 second exercise runs with a countdown: blink reset, distance focus or eye movement.

03 · check in

Check in once a day

Five minutes after your active hours end, the watch offers three quick questions about your eyes and your screen day. It's optional and takes a few taps.

Your day on the wrist

See the habit form.

Open Today on the watch: every break of the work day is a square. Green is done, red is skipped, grey is missed. Below it you see how much you did and your current streak.

Nothing to sync, and nothing to sign up for.

Questions

Before you install

Will it buzz during my meetings?

It buzzes only inside the active hours you set. In this version it doesn’t detect meetings. If a buzz comes at a bad moment, answer No. The Today screen shows it as skipped, not missed.

Which watches does it support?

The main current Garmin models that support Connect IQ apps. The full list is on the store page.

Does it use my heart rate or other sensors?

No. LookFar uses only a timer and vibration. It doesn't depend on your movement, sleep or workouts.

Is it free?

Yes, LookFar is free, with no ads and no account. A paid version may come later.

Is this a medical app?

No. LookFar is a break reminder. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything. If something about your eyes worries you, see an eye care professional.

Where is my data?

Your settings, your break history and, if you use it, the evening check-in. It all stays on your watch for 30 days, and nothing is sent anywhere. Details are on the privacy page.
