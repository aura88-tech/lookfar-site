LookFar

Privacy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

LookFar is a break reminder app for Garmin watches. In short: it keeps a small history of your breaks on your watch, and it doesn't send it anywhere.

What the app stores

Your break history: when each break happened and whether you did it, skipped it or missed it. The app uses it to show the Today screen and your streak.

Your settings, such as work hours and the reminder interval, so the app knows when to remind you.

All of this stays on your watch.

What the app doesn't collect

No name, email or account. No location. No heart rate or other sensor data. No contacts, no ads and no analytics. We don't sell or share anything, because we don't receive anything.

Garmin

You install LookFar and change its settings through Garmin's Connect IQ Store and Garmin Connect. Garmin handles that part under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Uninstall LookFar from your watch. Its break history is removed with it.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. Fonts load from Google Fonts. The site has no cookies and no tracking of its own.
