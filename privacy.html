LookFar

Privacy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

LookFar is a Garmin Connect IQ watch app that reminds you to take short eye breaks. In short: it doesn't collect, send or share any personal data. Everything it stores stays on your watch.

What the app stores on your watch

Settings: reminder interval, active hours, on/off switches.

Reminder history: the time of each reminder and whether you completed the exercise, stopped it, or didn't answer. Kept for 30 days, then deleted automatically.

Evening check-in answers, if you use the optional check-in: your three answers per day. Kept for 30 days, then deleted automatically.

A short internal event log for troubleshooting: times and event names, no personal content.

This data lives in the app's private storage on the watch. The app can't read your health or activity data, contacts, messages or location, and it doesn't ask for those permissions. The only permission it requests is Background, which lets the watch wake the app on a schedule to show a reminder.

What leaves the watch

Nothing. The app has no network access, no analytics, no ads, no accounts and no third-party services. Because it collects nothing, there is nothing to sell or share.

Deleting your data

Uninstall LookFar from your watch and all its data is removed. Reminder history and check-in answers also expire after 30 days.

Garmin

LookFar is distributed through the Garmin Connect IQ Store and runs on Garmin devices. Garmin may process information about downloads and device use under its own privacy policy.

Not a medical app

LookFar is a reminder and exercise timer for general wellbeing. It is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice. The app isn't directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. Fonts load from Google Fonts. The site has no cookies and no tracking of its own.
